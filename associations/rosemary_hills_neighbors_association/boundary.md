The boundaries of the Association are defined as follows: beginning from the point at
which the northern edge of the East-West Highway right-of-way intersects with the property
line that separates the Barrington Apartments from the houses on the east side of Leonard
Drive, the Association’s boundary extends north along that line to the point that it
reaches the southern edge of the CSX/Purple Line right-of-way; northwest along the
southern edge of that right-of-way to the point that it reaches the center of Lanier
Drive; south along the center of Lanier Drive and its right-of-way to the point that it
reaches the southern edge of the properties on the south side of Richland Place; east
along those property lines to the point that it reaches the southwest property line of the
property known as 2218 Richland Street; southeast along that property line to the point
that it reaches the western edge of the properties on the western side of Mark Court;
south along those property lines to the point that it reaches the northern edge of the
East-West Highway right-of-way; and east along the northern edge of that right-of-way to
the point of origin.
