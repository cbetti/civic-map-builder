Starting at the intersection of Dale Drive and Colesville Road, Woodside Park is the area
on the west side of Colesville Road to Spring Street, on the east/north side of Spring
Street to Georgia Avenue, on the east side of Georgia Avenue to Columbia Boulevard, on the
south side of Columbia Boulevard to Dale Drive, lots on both sides of Dale Drive to
Clement Road, lots on both sides of Clement Road for one block to Clement Place, lots on
both sides of the one block of Clement Place.  Back at the intersection of Clement Road
and Clement Place, continue with lots on both sides of Dale Drive to the beginning at the
intersection of Dale Drive and Colesville Road.
